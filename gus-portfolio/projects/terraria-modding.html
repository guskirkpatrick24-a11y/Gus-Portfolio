About the project

In 2023 I experimented with modding the classic 2D survival game Terraria, creating a sword and 3 frog-themed items in collaboration with the Rainy Lune plush brand out of Arizona.

My favorite is the Sonny the Frog staff, which summons a minion version of one of their plushies to fight for you. The code is C# and the sprite work is from Aseprite.
