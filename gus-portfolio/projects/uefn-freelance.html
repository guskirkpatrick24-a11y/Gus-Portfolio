How it started

In late 2023 I got into UEFN recreationally with my background in UE5. After GDC I found myself working on Road King Tycoon, which led to a multitude of contracts, collaborations and investment into the UGC space.

How it's going

As of Q4 2025 I've contributed to 20+ Fortnite maps in various roles and shipped maps of my own like Fort Souls: Boss Fight. My designs have been enjoyed by millions of people and generated hundreds of millions of impressions.

Skills I've gained

  • Game design: understanding genres and rapidly prototyping and iterating on them collaboratively to industry standards.
  • Programming: proficient in Verse and Unreal, using cutting-edge features like Scene Graph and multiplayer persistence.
  • Level design: faster iteration let me build maps for a variety of genres and player types.
  • Production: working in many roles taught me what goes into each part of a game — which let me lead Fort Souls from production as well as development.
  • Environment art, VFX and lighting: skills I love, with more time and agency to pursue them in UEFN.
  • UGC & QA knowledge: what players actually want to engage with, versus what developers think they want.
